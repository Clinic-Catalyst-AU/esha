Our Philosophy

Where clinical experience

At Esha Medi Spa in Kyneton, every treatment begins with a thorough consultation and a single guiding principle: your face, your story. Amanda Savis brings over 25 years of registered nursing experience - from ICU to aged care - to a boutique setting where nothing is rushed and nothing is off the shelf.

Here, you won't find a treatment menu pushed at every turn. You'll receive a tailored plan, honest advice, and clinical precision in a space designed to feel like the Macedon Ranges itself - unhurried, grounded, and quietly beautiful.

Esha is AHPRA-registered and operates under the full scope of cosmetic nursing regulations. Your safety is the foundation of every decision made inside this clinic.

Meet Your Practitioner

Amanda Savis

Amanda began her nursing career over 25 years ago and has worked across intensive care, urgent care, and aged care settings. She holds a Bachelor of Nursing and a Graduate Diploma in Midwifery from La Trobe University, and completed her Diploma of Cosmetic Nursing in 2021.

In 2022, Amanda was recognised with a Juv'ae Award - an industry accolade for excellence in cosmetic nursing. Her approach combines clinical rigour with a deep respect for individuality. She believes cosmetic treatment should enhance who you already are, not transform you into someone different.
